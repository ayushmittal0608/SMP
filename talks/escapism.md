# Escapism

We sometimes think escapism is to escape from the illusions that life has created for us, for eg, if we work too hard to achieve something, then our expectation is our achievement which drives us to work more and we get into the illusion of doing more work. But when we escape from this illusion, we get into another kind of illusion where we are spending more and more money on our lifestyle and buying expensive stuff which is another form of illusion, and one can escape from it when they deviate their mind to do work but that is tied to money and achievement too, so how can one escape this illusion. This is a very important question that we all have in our life where we often get stuck.

Now, who is the best in escapism? I think the best person that has sorted their life and has escaped from every illusion could be the one who picks a certain domain that he has chosen, maybe he doesn't have any expertise in that domain and is new to explore but think about themselves as someone who focus on the art being involved in doing it, for eg, I am assuming that I want to be a high IQ person and I am solving competitive programming questions, analyzing patterns, playing chess, sudoku and solving rubric cubes, so that art of making us a high IQ individual which can solve any problem if done these things would continuously develop a habit of loving their art and becoming good at it.

Let me give you another example, for eg, I am assuming that I want to be a highly creative person, so I start building something creative in react.js, enjoying music, craft and anything involving beauty, so I build neomorphism, glassmorphism, etc, and learn about elements of music, chords, scales, etc. and architectural calculations to build some craft on paper, etc. and sometimes we get into poetry, so now that love towards art and for being that art, we would continuously develop a habit of loving our creative side and becoming more and more creative.

For eg, I am assuming that I am a highly outspoken person, so I start speaking to people, having conversations, maybe some people would like it or not but we constantly seek feedback and ask people to help us improve our communication, so we often get to meet rude people, nice people and so on to whom we talk and we notice the pattern on how each one of them react.

In all these examples, there are things that can cause us to overthink and feel deeply without channeling our illusion for better good. For eg, we might think that since we are solving too much complex problem, still we are not getting any benefit out of it, nobody acknowledges us to solve problems despite efforts, etc. similarly, we keep on building something creative and still we are left with no one valuing our art and degrading our value of being creative, or if someone mocks us for our bad communication, so we might feel we aren't able to communicate ever and try to disconnect from the world.

So, the problem doesn't lie in us, it lies in our assumption of being not good at something, but imperfection is the way this universe has been created, imperfection leads to creativity, imperfection is what drives us to solve problem because we can only solve problem if we have seen imperfection in something and problem is equivalent to imperfection in something, similarly, imperfection at communication is what drives perfect communication because that leads to what we deeply feel and care about, rather than being someone or something else.

But at some point of time, we need to embrace the laws of nature and responsibilities and need to love things that we have chosen with that feeling of being something, else it would get left in the way and we lose our art. So, we might get through a series of imperfections but we can't become perfect and when we realize that we are imperfect, we start organizing things within us and a sense of discipline to be perfect comes and that discipline never comes but soon we start respecting the laws of nature otherwise we get stuck in jumping from one thing to the other without enjoying the art of something that has its way all upto the path that we want to go in.

So, discipline is required in our life but we need to know the path of escapism too and that perfect balance of discipline wherever required and escapism from it where not required is how one can orchestrate the illusion for the better good, and imperfection with the feeling and urge to be perfect is what drives us to being in discipline but expectation of outcome kills our journey to love our art and we could fall in the trap of illusion.
